DriveCAST reuses recorded driving interactions in new environments while preserving the spatial and temporal conditions that make each event meaningful.

01 · ABSTRACT

Transferring recorded driving scenarios to new environments does not necessarily preserve interaction conditions, and the original images and ego actions may no longer be suitable for training. DriveCAST addresses this with two components: Inter2Scene reconstructs interactions under road and kinematic constraints, while Scene2BEV converts the reconstructed scene into features compatible with a camera-derived representation. A shared planner learns from transferred experience without rendering new camera images or changing the camera input pathway at inference.

02 · METHOD

Transfer the interaction,
not just the trajectory.

DriveCAST separates what should remain fixed, what should be preserved, and what must be generated again.

01

Inter2Scene

Adjusts surrounding vehicle position, speed, and timing while keeping the target road, current ego state, and ego history fixed.

02

Reference rollout

Generates new ego supervision in simulation, so the transferred conditions are not tied to the recorded ego response.

03

Scene2BEV

Maps structured road and vehicle histories into BEV features compatible with the frozen camera encoder and shared planner.
